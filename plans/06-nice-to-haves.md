# Nice-to-haves (backlog beyond the MVP)

Roughly ordered by value to users.

## Selling

- **Sell assistant**: generate eBay listing titles and descriptions with the photos.
- **Silver lots**: group silver coins into lots by pure weight, priced near melt.
- Melt-based offer sheets for dealers ("we pay 85 % of melt").

## Collection value

- **Live portfolio value** following metal spot prices.
- Value history per collection.
- Alerts: "silver up 10 %, your silver is now worth €X".

## Collecting

- **Completion tracking**: "18 of 24 German €2 commemoratives".
- Want lists and swap lists (duplicates).
- Alerts for known **rare varieties, mint errors and key dates**.

## Accuracy and trust

- Optional **weight entry** (kitchen scale) → warn about possible fakes or wrong ID.
- Diameter from the capture sheet shown next to the catalogue diameter.
- Rough grade estimate with a clear disclaimer.
- Confidence shown per coin; "ask an expert" button.

## Convenience

- Quick **single-coin scan** mode (competitor parity).
- Video mode: flip coins in front of the camera.
- **Shared family collections** (several heirs looking at one box).
- Read-only share links for dealers.
- German and English, later more languages.
- Google Sheets export, Numista collection import if supported.

## Business

- Dealer mode: multiple customers, branded reports.
- Printed capture sheets shipped with coin capsules.
- Own classifier trained on confirmed coins (with consent) to cut LLM costs.
