# Monetization

## Unit costs

- LLM: measured $13.22 for 1,665 coins with the Batch API → ~$0.008/coin.
  Synchronous (Express) ~2× → ~$0.016/coin.
- Catalogue: Numista terms unknown (see [01-market.md](01-market.md)). Image-search plan
  starts at €100/month minimum. Text search ~1.2 requests per coin amortised in our run.
- Storage and compute: small.

→ Roughly **€0.01–0.02 per coin** plus a fixed catalogue fee. High margins at almost any price.

## Pricing

Most users have a one-off need (inherited box), so one-time passes beat subscriptions.
This also sets us apart from the subscription-heavy clone apps.

| Tier | Price idea | For | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | €0 | Trying it out, marketing | 25–30 coins, full export, Economy processing |
| Collection pass (one-time) | €19 up to 1,000 coins, €39 up to 5,000 | Inheritors, main market | All exports, Express processing, PDF report, 12 months access |
| Collector | ~€5/month | Ongoing cataloguing | Unlimited collections, live value tracking, alerts, completion lists |
| Dealer / Pro | ~€49/month | Dealers, estate clearers | Multiple customer collections, melt-based offers, branded PDF, bulk export |

Prices are guesses; test them with the first real users.

## Extra revenue

- **eBay Partner Network**: commission on "sell" / "see sold listings" links.
- **Bullion dealer referrals**: referral fee for silver/gold lots sent to a partner dealer.
- **Grading service referrals** (PCGS / NGC) for coins worth grading.
- **Supplies**: printed capture sheets, coin capsules, albums (affiliate or own shop).
- **Paid expert review** of the top coins (later, with a partner numismatist).

No ads inside the app.

## Marketing

- Highlights page ([04](04-data-and-highlights.md)) as the main hook ("X kg of silver found").
- Before/after content: box of coins → spreadsheet with values.
- SEO: "inherited coin collection what is it worth", "Münzsammlung geerbt Wert".
- Communities: coin forums and subreddits, estate / inheritance groups.
- German and English from day one.

## Liability

- Values are estimates; disclaimer on every export and in the terms of use.
- Never claim authenticity; flag possible fakes only as "check this".
